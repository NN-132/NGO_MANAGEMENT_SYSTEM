MISSION

1. Empower individuals and communities to take control of their lives and futures by providing them with the necessary resources, education, and support.

2. Inspire individuals and communities to become agents of change, encouraging active participation and leadership in our shared mission.

3. Raise awareness and educate the public about hunger, its implications, and the actions needed to address it effectively.

VISION

1. Sustainability is the norm, with our planet thriving through responsible stewardship, and future generations inheriting a world that is healthier and more resilient.

2. Hope and Possibility abound, inspiring individuals and communities to dream big, act boldly, and create a brighter, more inclusive future for all.

3. Equality and Justice prevail, where every individual's rights are protected, and all have equal opportunities for growth and prosperity.

ACCOMPLISHMENTS

1. Is the main information provider for Bangladeshi nonprofits, publishing weekly electronic newsletter, quarterly NGO magazine, electronic newsletters in English and Bangla in every two months, handbooks and leaflets.

2. Launched a number of development projects like co-operation with local governments, good governance for Board development, philanthropy development with businesses, mapping and training Bangladeshi umbrella organizations, etc.

3. Carries out training programs for NGOs and public sector representatives on different topics related to civil society and cooperation between NGOs and public sector.
